## Description

A ticket management system built with TypeScript, Bun and Tanstack Router and Drizzle ORM for database interactions. This project allowed me to deepen my understanding of TypeScript and Bun, and also while learning team collaboration and project management, and new technologies.

## What I learned

- More in depth knowledge of TypeScript and Bun
- Team collaboration and project management
- Tanstack Router for routing
